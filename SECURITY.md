If you discover a security vulnerability within this repository or any of my associated projects, please do not disclose it publicly.

Please report it by sending an email to [subhammallick454@gmail.com](mailto:subhammallick454@gmail.com).

I will endeavor to respond to your report as quickly as possible and coordinate the fix and disclosure process.
