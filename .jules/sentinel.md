## 2024-05-22 - [Added Security Policy]
**Vulnerability:** Missing vulnerability reporting channel
**Learning:** Repositories without a `SECURITY.md` risk public disclosure of vulnerabilities in their issue tracker, exposing users to unnecessary risk before a patch is ready. Adding a `SECURITY.md` establishes a safe, private channel for reporting.
**Prevention:** Always include a `SECURITY.md` file in new and existing repositories to ensure security issues are reported securely and confidentially.
